The suite

Three addons, one minimap menu.

01/rp

RICO PLATES

Animated health bars, player and target portraits, party alerts and a cast warning that tracks spells aimed at you. When you cast a heal, a green extension shows the expected healing on the recipient's health bar.

  • Square minimap, clock and area overview
  • Combined inventory /ri
  • Mining and quest guidance
02/rl

RICO LOOT

Search by zone, creature or item. Compare the available reference and observation data, and record drops as you play.

  • Searchable loot tables
  • Local loot observations
  • Item wishlist and farming goals
03/rf

RICO FISHING

Cast and reel in with one key, find fishing spots, and see what you caught in each session.

  • One-Key Fishing on F
  • Catch log /rf log
  • Fishing guide, map tools and goals

New in RicoFishing 1.4.0

One-Key Fishing.

Equip a fishing pole and use F for the whole loop. Each cast and reel-in takes its own keypress. Take the pole off to return to your usual F binding.

Open /rf log to see quantities by item in your current session and up to 25 previous sessions for that character.

  1. F
    Cast your lineStand by fishable water with your pole equipped.
  2. F
    Reel in at the splashPress when the bobber splashes. Fishing loot is collected automatically.
  3. F
    Cast againPress again for the next catch.

Setup

Install Rico Suite.

The release ZIP includes all three addons for World of Warcraft: Forever.

  1. Download the release

    Get RicoSuite-forever.zip from the GitHub release page.

  2. Extract the addon folders

    Close WoW and extract the three folders into _forever_/Interface/AddOns.

  3. Open the Rico menu

    Start WoW and open the suite with the Rico minimap button or the slash commands above.

To update, repeat the first two steps with the latest release.
